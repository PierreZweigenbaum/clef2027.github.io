TalentCLEF is an evaluation lab that aims to establish a public benchmark and collaborative forum for assessing NLP models in Human Capital Management, promoting fairness, explainability, multilingual capability, and cross-industry adaptability to advance skill and job intelligence technologies.

<!--more-->

## Tasks

The TalentCLEF Lab features two different tasks:

{{< accordion  open_all="true" >}}
  {{< accordion-item title="Contextualized Job-Person Matching" >}}
  Rank candidate profiles for each job offer using synthetic, context-rich job and profile descriptions in English, Spanish and French. Subtask 1.1 (Candidate Ranking) covers monolingual, cross-lingual and bias settings; Subtask 1.2 (Explainable Candidate Ranking) also requires a grounded explanation for each top-ranked candidate.
  {{< /accordion-item >}}
  {{< accordion-item title="Paragraph-to-Skill Ranking under Negation" >}}
  Given a short job-market paragraph, rank the ESCO skills by polarity, distinguishing skills that are required from those the text negates, defers or excludes.
  {{< /accordion-item >}}
{{< /accordion >}}
